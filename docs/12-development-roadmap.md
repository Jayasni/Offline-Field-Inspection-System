# 12 — Ten Phases
1. Setup: frontend/backend/PostgreSQL/Docker.
2. Authentication: password hashing, JWT, refresh, RBAC.
3. Dashboards and assignments: role-specific UI, user/assignment management.
4. Inspections: checklist, remarks, lifecycle and versioning.
5. Offline DB: Dexie schema, repositories and durable drafts.
6. Photos: camera, Blob storage, upload validation.
7. PWA: manifest, service worker and connectivity.
8. Sync: outbox, retry, idempotency and conflict handling.
9. Integration/security: ownership, validation, audit and end-to-end wiring.
10. Tests/release: automated tests, deployment, HTTPS, backups and documentation.
